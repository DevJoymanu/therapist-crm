Introduction

Welcome to Yanrol Systemic Family Counselling Services & Therapy. This consent form outlines the terms and conditions for our counselling services. Please read this document carefully. You have the right to ask questions before signing, refuse certain techniques and terminate counselling at any time. Counselling can be beneficial but may also involve discussing difficult emotions or experiences.

Confidentiality

  • All our counselling sessions are confidential.
  • What you say here will not be disclosed to anyone, except as required by law when:
    • — Someone is hurting you
    • — You want to hurt yourself
    • — You want to hurt someone
    • — You give written permission to share with another trusted adult
  • We maintain records of your sessions securely and well protected.

Session Details

  • Sessions will be 45 to 60 minutes long.
  • The frequency and duration of sessions will depend on your needs.

Cancellation Policy

  • 24 hour notice is required for cancellation of a booked session.

Communication

  • We may communicate via phone and/or email for scheduling and updates.

Payment Terms

  • We accept payment via cash, bank transfer and mobile money transfers.
  • Payment must be received before the start of each session.

Consent

By signing below, you consent to participate in counselling services with Yanrol Systemic Family Counselling Services & Therapy.
